The Usher adapter's work for the category model begins, against a local draft of Usher's coming record registration, whose categories are named SQON conditions.

- `modules/usher-adapter/src/conditionFields.ts`: a startup check of every category condition against the catalogue's index mapping. Each field it names resolves through the mapping, multi-fields included, outside any nested mapping, as a keyword, date or numeric field, with ranges only on dates and numbers; SQON's operator aliases count as the operators they stand for. A date field names no format, or one built only from `strict_date_optional_time`, `date_optional_time` and `epoch_millis`. Each value a condition holds is one its field's type holds: a non-empty string on a keyword, a finite number on a numeric field, and on a date a full date or a full date-time to the millisecond on a real calendar day, and a date `between` lists the bound starting earlier first, so a condition holds only what the independent checker models. Not yet wired into startup, which waits for Usher's released type.
- `modules/usher-adapter/src/draftRegistration.ts`: the local draft of that registration type.
