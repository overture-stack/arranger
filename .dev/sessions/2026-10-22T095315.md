Main gains the download route's rules, and a roadmap item for saved sets of any size, after the GraphQL error rule.

- `.dev/roadmap.md`, `.dev/docs/atlas/roadmap/sets-feature.md`: a new item, "Saved sets of any size usable as filters": a set's ids stored in chunks of at most a configurable size, a set filter combining one lookup per chunk in one query, and a startup refusal of a chunk size above a data index's `index.max_terms_count`.
- `modules/graphql-router/src/download/index.js`, `utils/dataToExportFormat.js`, `utils/getAllData.js`: a body the form parser refuses is answered with its rule's fixed text, as plain text, with the parser's own `4xx`; a successful download carries `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`; an export ends with its error when a row cannot be formatted, in JSON as in TSV; a client leaving mid-stream is logged at info as `download.client_disconnected`, and a search failing after the stream was destroyed is logged once as `download.stream_failed`.
- `docs/reference/08-Migration/v3.1.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `modules/graphql-router/README.md`: the parser's refusals and the export's row errors for a 3.0.x deployment, and the success header and logging.
- `.dev/tech-debt.md`: "Download route body is brittle" records items 3, 4 and 5 done; new entries for a disabled route answering in JSON, an export and a search treating a disabled client filter differently, a source-less hit exported differently in TSV and JSON, and a malformed client filter to be refused as the client's mistake.
- `modules/graphql-router/src/loopbackServer.fixture.ts`, the router's route tests, `network/remoteNodeStub.fixture.ts`: every test server listens on 127.0.0.1 at a free port through one shared helper, closed when its exchange ends, and a remote node nothing answers at holds its own loopback port for the whole test, closing every connection unanswered, so no other local listener can answer a test's request. `.dev/tech-debt.md`: the entry for route tests colliding with other local listeners is done and removed.
- `modules/graphql-router/src/middleware/utils/esFilter.js`, `middleware/buildQuery/index.js`, `mapping/getLegacyDateFieldNames.ts`, `mapping/addMappingsToTypes.ts`, the resolvers, `utils/getAllData.js`, `middleware/buildAggregations/`: a date alone in a filter means its whole day. On a default-format date field it reaches the engine as written, which reads it whole; on a field whose format holds `yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm:ss.SSSSSS`, each operator's date alone is written as the day's first or last instant, `between` from the first to the last, and `in`, `not-in`, `some-not-in` and `all` over the day's span. Each catalogue's legacy-pattern date fields are read from its mapping once and reach every compile, facets included.
- `integration-tests/server/test/dateAlone.test.ts`: `gt`, `gte`, `lt`, `lte`, `between`, `in` (alone and beside a date-time) and `not-in`, on a default-format and a legacy-pattern field, with and without a nesting prefix, through hits, a facet, a saved set and an export, on both engines; `some-not-in`, `all` and nested fields are covered by the unit tests.
- `docs/reference/08-Migration/v3.1.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `docs/reference/04-sqon-in-detail.md`: a date alone covers its whole day in UTC, on a default-format field and on one whose format holds the legacy pattern; the migration guide names filters holding a date alone in `gt` or `lte`, and a format spelling the pattern differently. `.dev/tech-debt.md`: a date facet's day and a typed date's day must be the same day.
