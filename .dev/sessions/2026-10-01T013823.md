Restored exports for integrations without access control, and made every export entry point take its filter from the router's recorded decision rather than from an argument threaded through layers.

- `modules/graphql-router/src/accessControl/`: `includeEverything`, with `getDefaultServerSideFilter` kept as its deprecated alias; the router checks its `getServerSideFilter` at construction and records its decision on every request context it builds; `resolveServerSideFilter` gives an export the router's filter when the caller passes none, combines a caller's filter with it under `and`, and refuses a context no router built. Filter callbacks are synchronous, and any failure becomes an `AccessControlError`, answered with fixed text on GraphQL and in network search's node errors.
- `modules/graphql-router/src/download/`, `utils/getAllData.js`, `utils/dataToExportFormat.js`: `dataStream` and `getAllData` validate their own input (one file, fields picked by name, typed values, sort entries naming a field and an `asc` or `desc` order) and refuse before any output; failures answer as plain text with fixed wording, 400 for invalid input and 500 otherwise; paging uses `search_after` without a separate count query and stops when the reader goes away. `AccessControlError` and `InvalidExportRequestError` are exported so an integration's own export route can tell them apart.
- `modules/graphql-router/src/middleware/buildQuery/`: error messages carry no client input.
- `apps/search-server/src/configs/fromEnv/`: `ENABLE_ACCESS_CONTROL` is parsed strictly, defaults to no access control and logs its source; `true` refuses startup until the Usher adapter exists, as does any unrecognized value or a contradiction with a host's `filters` option.
- `modules/types/src/tools/stringFns.ts`: boolean flags accept `true`, `false`, `1` and `0`; any other value is ignored with a warning and the flag keeps its default.
- `tsconfig.release.json` in four packages: published declarations keep their TSDoc.
- `integration-tests/server/`: the access-control fixture now stores its denied document, so the search, aggregation and new download tests fail if a filter is dropped.
- `modules/graphql-router/README.md`, `docs/reference/07-feature-flags.md`, `.dev/docs/atlas/layers-and-vocabulary.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`: why a deployment without access control passes nothing, export routes as public API, `ENABLE_ACCESS_CONTROL` and the boolean value rule, the recorded states, and the release notes, including corrections to entries describing rc.6.
- `.dev/roadmap.md`: vulnerability disclosure as a step of the release workflow.
