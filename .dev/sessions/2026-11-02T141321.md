ENABLE_ACCESS_ENFORCEMENT governs access enforcement alone, and a server-side filter is a separate thing.

- `apps/search-server/src/configs/fromEnv/enableAccessControl.ts`, `aggregator.ts`: with `false`, as with the variable unset, an embedding application's `filters` function applies. With `true`, a `filters` or a `getRequester` function is refused at startup, and `ENABLE_ACCESS_CONTROL`, the variable's earlier name, refuses startup whenever it is set. The startup log reports access enforcement, and how the variable decided it, apart from where the server-side filter comes from, under `access_enforcement.switch` and `server_side_filter.image_source`.
- `enableAccessControl.test.ts`: each combination of the variable with a `filters` and a `getRequester` function, the earlier name set, the two startup lines, and the refusals' text.
- `modules/graphql-router/src/router.ts`, `accessControl/accessControlRecord.ts`: each catalogue's startup line reads `server-side filter: configured`, `none (defaulted)` or `none (explicit)`, under `server_side_filter.startup`.
- `docs/reference/07-feature-flags.md`, `docs/reference/08-Migration/v3.1.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `modules/graphql-router/README.md`, `.dev/docs/atlas/layers-and-vocabulary.md`: the tables and the entries, to match, and the release candidates' names listed beside their replacements.
- `.dev/tech-debt.md`: a deployment's own data scope beside Usher's access enforcement.
- `modules/usher-adapter/src/requester.ts`, `createUsherAccessControl.ts`, `fixtures/results.ts`: the bridge's principal is read by its `credential`. `requesterOf` answers a confirmed credential as a principal, a suspended one as suspended with the subject kept for attribution alone, an unconfirmed one as suspended, and none as anonymous. Each `access_enforcement` event carries `credential` beside `userId`, the bridge's `subjectToLog` of the principal (`README.md`, `.dev/docs/arranger-auth/usher-adapter-tests.md`).
