The application's key is `USHER_KEY`, and the key and the audience both come from the application's registration with Usher's controller.

- `apps/search-server/src/configs/fromEnv/usherBridgeEnv.ts`, `apps/search-server/.env.schema`, `docs/reference/07-feature-flags.md`: `USHER_APPLICATION_KEY` is `USHER_KEY`, in its alphabetical place. Its entry says the controller shows the key once at registration, one per application, kept out of version control, with whitespace around it ignored, and no longer says how to produce one. `USHER_AUDIENCE` is the identifier the controller generated at registration.
- `usherBridgeEnv.test.ts`: the bridge's key is read from `USHER_KEY` and passed on unchanged.
